AEGIS

Asteroid Exploration & Gravitational Interior Surveyor

Cranfield University Group Design Project

Mission Overview

AEGIS investigates a mission to rendezvous with near-Earth asteroids, refine their orbital and physical properties, and demonstrate controlled ion beam deflection for planetary defence.
